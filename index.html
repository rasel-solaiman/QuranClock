About this app

Quran Clock Widget is a beautiful and functional application designed to display Quranic verses and the current time directly on your phone's home screen. The core mission of this app is to help you stay connected with the words of Allah throughout your day.

Key Features

  • Beautiful Widgets: Aesthetic and user-friendly Quran widgets for your home screen.
  • Quranic Verses: Each widget beautifully displays Quranic verses along with their translations.
  • Time & Verse Integration: Seamlessly combines the current time with meaningful verses.
  • Easy Customization: Options to toggle between 24-hour time format and display or hide the Arabic verse.
  • Auto-Update: Supports automatic widget updates at set intervals.

How to use:

  1. Long-press on your home screen to add the widget manually.
  2. Select 'Widgets' and locate 'Quran Clock'.
  3. Drag and drop the widget onto your home screen.
  4. For optimal performance and regular updates, disable battery optimization for this app in your phone settings.
  5. You can resize the widget to allow for automatic text scaling.
