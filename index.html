About this app

Quran Clock Widget is a beautiful and functional application designed to display Quranic verses and the current time directly on your phone's home screen. The core mission of this app is to help you stay connected with the words of Allah throughout your day.

Screenshots

Key Features

  • Beautiful Widgets

    Aesthetic and user-friendly designs for your home screen.

  • Quranic Verses

    Displays rich Quranic verses along with their proper translations.

  • Time & Verse Integration

    Seamlessly combines current time with meaningful daily verses.

  • Easy Customization

    Toggle between 24-hour formats and hide/show Arabic texts instantly.

  • Auto-Update

    Supports automatic widget updates at customized intervals.

How to use

  1. 1. Long-press on your home screen to add the widget manually.
  2. 2. Select 'Widgets' and locate 'Quran Clock'.
  3. 3. Drag and drop the widget onto your home screen.
  4. 4. For optimal performance, disable battery optimization for this app in settings.
  5. 5. You can resize the widget to allow for automatic text scaling.
